# Data sources

Staged or generated by build_notebook.py so the notebook is self-contained.

- gnk_kl_flow_vs_gaussian.csv: copied from the g-and-k v3 summary.
- gnk_kl_paired_per_seed.csv and gnk_paper_grid_bootstrap_ci.csv: paired-seed g-and-k KL values and bootstrap summaries.
- gnk_theta_oracle_by_n.csv: moment-matched Gaussian oracle row.
- gnk_hexadecile_gaussian.csv and gnk_hexadecile_flow.csv: hexadecile summary-dimension comparison.
- gnk_posterior_overlay.csv: KDE of selected g-and-k posterior draws.
- gnk_bsl_diagnostic.csv: dense-proposal BSL diagnostic table.
- gnk_robust_scaling_overlay.csv and gnk_robust_scaling_summary.json: n=1000 robust standardisation diagnostic.
- gnk_robust_scaling_n5000_2M_summary.json and gnk_robust_scaling_n5000_2M.png: n=5000 robust standardisation pilot.
- gnk_rejection_abc_summary.json: rejection-ABC Gaussian-NPE pilot.
- dim_scaling_pilot_kl_by_d.csv: partial dimension-scaling aggregate from available metrics under res/gnk_dim_scaling/.
- stereological_coverage.csv, stereological_bias_by_seed.csv, and stereological_posterior_overlay.csv: stereological summaries copied from the 2026-05-13 postprocessing cache.
- ma2_b0_kl.csv: stored MA(2) compatible-case KL medians, with current-reference entries from the latest refreshes where available.
- ma2_b0_flow_current_reference_per_seed.csv and ma2_b0_flow_current_reference_summary.csv: 80-seed MA(2) b0 flow-NPE current-reference refresh.
- ma2_b0_gaussian_current_reference_per_seed.csv: five-seed MA(2) b0 Gaussian-NPE current-reference audit.
- ma2_b0_reference_audit.csv: pilot MA(2) b0 reference-consistency audit used for seed-level comparison.
- ma2_delta1_refresh.csv and ma2_delta1_refresh_audit.csv: exact compatible MA(2) refresh summaries.
- ma2_compatibility_flow.csv and ma2_compatibility_gaussian.csv: delta0 compatibility sweep inputs.
- ma2_posterior_overlay_seed_22.csv and .png: seed-22 MA(2) overlay.
